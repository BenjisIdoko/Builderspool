# BuildersPool seller terms (DRAFT for founder review)

> **Status: draft. Not published.** This is a plain-language first draft written from the product rules in `docs/floot-port-notes.md`. It is **not legal advice**. Have a Nigerian lawyer review it before it is published. Items in [square brackets] are decisions or facts still to be confirmed. Version when published: `v1` (the `accounts.termsVersion` setting).

## 1. Who these terms are for

These terms apply to every business that sells through BuildersPool (a "Seller"). The person who creates a Seller account (the "Owner") accepts these terms for the whole business and is responsible for the staff they add.

## 2. Your account and verification

1. You must give true and complete details: business registration number, CAC number, ID, TIN, business address and bank details. Details must belong to your business.
2. We review every submission. We may approve it, reject it with a reason, or ask you to resubmit. We may suspend an approved account (for example for quality problems, missed deliveries or false information). While suspended you cannot bid or be awarded orders. Awards you had not yet accepted are released.
3. Keep your documents current. Tell us if an expiry date passes. We may pause your account if a required document expires.
4. Only the Owner can change verification details, bank details, these terms and the team. Staff can bid and manage orders on your behalf.
5. [If you change your bank details after approval, we re-check that the account name matches your registered business before we pay to the new account.]

## 3. Listings

1. A listing must show your real price, stock and lead time for a real product you can supply, with clear photos (at least [1000 px] on the shorter side, JPEG or PNG, up to [4 MB or 5 MB: to be aligned]).
2. Photos must not contain contact details, watermarks of another business, or misleading images.
3. Products that spoil or expire (such as cement or paint) need a production or expiry date, and must have at least [30 days] of life left when delivered.
4. Our automated checks may hold a listing for review (for example a price far from similar listings) or reject it with a reason. We may remove a listing that breaks these terms.

## 4. How selling works

**Buy Direct.** A buyer picks your listing. You have [24 hours] to accept. If you decline or do not respond in time, the buyer is refunded and the order does not count as a sale.

**Pool & Save.** Buyers' orders are pooled by category and delivery zone. Approved Sellers for that category and zone may submit **one sealed bid** for the whole request. You must price every item. A bid above an item's limit rules out your whole bid. You cannot see other bids. Bids cannot be changed once sent.

**Awards.** Bids are scored on price, performance, capacity, delivery speed, location and completeness. The top three are offered the request in turn, [one hour] each. If you accept, you agree to supply every item at your bid prices.

## 5. Delivery and quality

1. You deliver the goods to the BuildersPool fulfilment centre named on the order, in the quantity and quality you agreed.
2. The centre checks the goods and issues a goods received note when they pass. Goods that do not match the order may be rejected, and you may not be paid for them.
3. If you cannot supply an accepted order, tell us straight away. We may mark the order as not supplied, which can affect your score and your account.

## 6. Payment

1. Buyers pay into escrow when they order. **We pay you after the goods received note**, at your bid price (pool) or the listing price less our commission (Buy Direct).
2. Our commission on Buy Direct orders is [3%: rate not final].
3. We may hold a payment while we look into a problem with an order. We will tell you why.
4. [Seller payouts are not switched on yet. We will tell you before they start. Until then, receipts are recorded and nothing is lost.]
5. Payments go only to the bank account verified on your account.

## 7. Your conduct

1. Do not take buyers off the platform to avoid our fees, give false information, manipulate bids, or share another Seller's information.
2. You are responsible for what your staff do on your account. Remove staff who leave.
3. Follow Nigerian law, including tax and consumer protection rules, and keep the licences and standards your products need.

## 8. Our records and your data

We keep a record of important actions on the platform (bids, awards, receipts, payments and account decisions) and may use it to resolve disputes. We use your information to run the marketplace and to meet legal duties. [Privacy details to be added by the lawyer.]

## 9. Ending the relationship

You may close your account by telling us, once your accepted orders are delivered and paid. We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms. Money properly due for delivered and accepted goods is still paid.

## 10. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. When we do, we change the version and ask the Owner to accept it again. You may not bid or accept awards until you have accepted the new version.

## 11. Law and disputes

These terms are governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. [Dispute process and venue to be confirmed by the lawyer.]
